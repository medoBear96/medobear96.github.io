Bachelor’s thesis

Flywheel Pendulum

A naturally unstable pendulum stabilized by the reaction torque of an electronically controlled flywheel, from mathematical model to physical prototype.

ControlC++ElectronicsMechanics
Repository ↗

Inside the project

Control theory made physical.

The thesis moved from a mathematical model and controller design to an electromechanical prototype. A motor-driven flywheel generates the reaction torque required to correct the pendulum, joining sensing, actuation, structure design and experimental tuning in one closed loop.

08

Resource-efficient stereo FIR filtering

FPGA Audio Equalizer

A stereo audio filtering pipeline on Cyclone V FPGA: complementary 51-tap FIR high- and low-pass filters, selectable bypass and nine gain levels, implemented in VHDL around a folded, time-multiplexed MAC that uses a single DSP block.

FPGAVHDLDSPDigital filtersAudio
Not available

Inside the project

Two filters, one DSP block.

Designed for 48 kHz, 24-bit stereo audio, the project exploits FIR symmetry and a time-multiplexed multiply-accumulate state machine to minimize FPGA resources. The complete pipeline was verified against MATLAB, exercised in ModelSim with a VHDL testbench and measured on physical hardware with a PicoScope.
