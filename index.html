About

I have always had two passions: sailing and building things.

I work across embedded development, test automation and hardware integration. I am most comfortable where software has to interact with a real device, an imperfect sensor or a physical constraint.

I learn by making: understanding how a system behaves, finding what slows it down or makes it fragile, then building the tool that makes it simpler and more dependable.
